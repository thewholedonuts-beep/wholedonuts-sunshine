# Whole Donuts: the whole picture

Sunshine is an optional local companion to the Whole Donuts Universe. Start at
`wenevergonnaclose.com`; Sunshine does not replace its gateway.

## Start where you are

There is no single kind of person this is for. A maker, artist, trade professional, organizer, business leader, neighbor, or person rebuilding after a hard time can enter with what they know today. The first task is simple: name what you are creating, choose a figure, and take one next step.

## The pieces

| Piece | What it does |
|---|---|
| Whole Donuts Universe | The canonical gateway and source of wider experience. |
| Sunshine World | An optional local stick-figure companion that returns visitors to the canonical gateway. |
| Contribution route | A GitHub pull request route for original accessible improvements, templates, and stick-figure ideas. |

## What is a crumb?

A crumb is any useful piece someone can carry forward. It can be a lesson, a tool, a trade skill, a creative method, a contact, a resource, an encouragement, or a practical next step. A crumb does not need to come from a particular school, job, neighborhood, or life story to matter.

| Crumb | What it can do |
|---|---|
| A learned crumb | Gives someone a plain-language starting point. |
| A maker crumb | Turns an idea into a design, repair, service, or project. |
| A connection crumb | Introduces a person to a resource, opportunity, or collaborator. |
| A care crumb | Offers encouragement, time, presence, or a way through a hard moment. |
| A support crumb | Sustains shared work through voluntary money, materials, tools, or space. |

Share only what you have the right to share. Do not include someone else's private, financial, health, or identifying information without their clear permission.

## How people enter and grow

1. **Start at the canonical gateway.** Open `wenevergonnaclose.com`.
2. **Choose Sunshine deliberately.** Use its optional local World without an account.
3. **Contribute deliberately.** Open a GitHub pull request when you have original material that is ready to share.

Recognition marks a completed path. It never measures a person's worth, rank, or right to belong.

## Our promise

This is a place to ask, try, and finish what you started. Take what helps. Keep your own voice. Create the next project of positivity. Leave the door open for the person who comes after you.
