# Bring something to the table

Whole Donuts welcomes original stick figures, accessible improvements, useful templates, and practical community ideas.

## Two ways in

1. **Leave a crumb:** Use the reviewed submission form at [wenevergonnaclose.com/#make](https://wenevergonnaclose.com/#make). This is the easiest route for an idea, link, or short contribution.
2. **Open a pull request:** Fork the repository, make a focused change under `apps/public-site/`, and open a pull request explaining what the contribution adds.

## Stick-figure contributions

- Keep figures simple, welcoming, and recognizable without identity labels.
- Submit only work you made or have permission to contribute.
- Use SVG or the existing HTML/CSS/JavaScript drawing patterns.
- Preserve keyboard navigation, readable contrast, reduced-motion behavior, and useful alternative text.
- Do not add trackers, third-party embeds, private information, harassment, or copyrighted characters.

Every contribution is reviewed before publication. Participation does not guarantee publication, payment, promotion, or any other outcome.
